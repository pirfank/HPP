    About the Platform

    A Growing Indian Property Platform

    This platform uses machine-learning regression models to estimate property prices across India's major metros. Bengaluru is the first city with a live prediction model, trained on local housing data across location, area, BHK, and number of bathrooms.

    Results are estimates, not appraisals. Actual property prices can vary based on factors such as property condition, exact location, amenities, age of the building, and current market conditions.
